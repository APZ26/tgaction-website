We are your neighbours.

Residents, proud, protective, and united by one common cause: to defend our village from inappropriate development.

How we started

This group began as a handful of concerned residents. A few emails. A WhatsApp group. Then meetings around kitchen tables and in local pubs. That handful grew, and continues to grow.

Now, we are organising, researching and challenging. We're working with planning experts, engaging local councillors, and preparing to speak with one voice when the time comes.

What is proposed

Multiple developers have now set their sights on the Green Belt surrounding Tidbury Green, with proposals totalling around 1,700+ homes on the land south of Norton Lane and beyond. This wave of development would triple the size of Tidbury Green, urbanise protected Green Belt, and bring lasting disruption to the way of life we all cherish.

It's not just fields on a map. It's our outlook, our buffer from flooding, our rural identity, and for those of us living opposite, it's quite literally our home.

Why we object

These developments are premature. None of these sites is allocated for housing in the draft Local Plan, and the Council itself describes these applications as speculative. Yet they are being aggressively pushed forward, piecemeal, by competing developers, without any assessment of their combined impact and without meaningful consultation with the people who will live in their shadow.

We've watched other villages lose their green spaces and community spirit. We will not let Tidbury Green become the next casualty.

With facts. With unity. With heart.

We do this not just for ourselves, but for the future of Tidbury Green: for our children, for our wildlife, for our community.
