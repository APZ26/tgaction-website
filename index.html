The right homes. The right place.

We are not anti-housing. We believe new homes must be balanced with the infrastructure and services needed to support them. Roads, schools, healthcare and drainage must all be considered before development is approved. Yet these proposals remain unallocated in the draft Local Plan, have been described by the Council as speculative, and have not been assessed for their combined impact. Good planning means getting the balance right — the right homes, in the right places, with the right infrastructure.
